Northern Airlines Customer Service Policy
1. Cancellations and Refunds
Northern Airlines offers three types of tickets: Basic, Standard, and Premium.
Basic tickets cannot be refunded or changed.
Standard tickets can be canceled before departure for travel credit, with a $75 cancellation fee. Cash refunds are not available.
Premium tickets can be fully refunded to the original payment method if canceled at least two hours before departure. Cancellations made less than two hours before departure will receive travel credit with a $50 cancellation fee.
Regardless of ticket type, customers can receive a full refund within 24 hours of purchasing their ticket, as long as the flight was booked at least seven days in advance.
Customers who miss their flight without canceling beforehand will lose the full value of their ticket.
Travel credits expire after 12 months and cannot be transferred to another person.
2. Family Loss and Emergency Travel
Customers traveling due to the death of an immediate family member may receive a 20% discount on the base price of a Standard or Premium ticket.
Immediate family includes parents, children, siblings, grandparents, spouses, and legal guardians.
The discount must be requested and approved before purchasing the ticket. Proof of death must be provided within 14 days of travel.
The discount cannot be applied to a ticket that has already been purchased, even if the flight has not taken place yet.
All normal cancellation and baggage policies still apply. The discount cannot be combined with other promotions.
Customers who cannot provide the required documents on time should speak with a customer service representative.

All customers are allowed one free personal item.

Premium customers get their first checked bag free. Their second checked bag still costs $50.
Checked bags can weigh up to 50 pounds and measure up to 62 inches in total length, width, and height.
Bags weighing more than 50 pounds but no more than 70 pounds cost an additional $75.
Bags measuring more than 62 inches but no more than 80 inches cost an additional $100.
If a bag is both too heavy and too large, both fees apply on top of the regular baggage fee.
Bags over 70 pounds or 80 inches will not be accepted.
Free baggage allowances cannot be shared with other passengers, even if they are traveling together.
4. Changing Flights
Basic tickets cannot be changed voluntarily.
Standard customers can change their flight before departure for a $50 fee, plus any increase in the ticket price.
Premium customers do not have to pay a change fee but must still cover any increase in the ticket price.
If the new flight is cheaper, Premium customers will receive the difference as travel credit. Standard customers will not receive credit for the difference.
Changing a flight does not restart the 24 hour refund period.
5. Flight Cancellations and Delays
If Northern Airlines cancels a flight, customers can choose to be booked on another flight at no additional cost or receive a full refund for the canceled flight and any unused services purchased for it. This applies to all ticket types.
If a flight is delayed by three hours or more for reasons within the airline's control, customers are eligible for a $15 meal voucher.
If the delay requires an overnight stay and is within the airline's control, Northern Airlines will cover up to $150 per hotel room.
Meal vouchers and hotel accommodations are not provided for weather related delays.
Customers who accept a replacement flight after a cancellation cannot also receive a refund for their original flight.
6. Customer Service and Special Requests
Customers with unusual circumstances, disputed charges, missing documents, or questions not covered by this policy should speak with a customer service representative.
A representative may review these situations individually, but an exception or refund is not guaranteed.
The chatbot can answer questions about company policies but cannot change reservations, approve exceptions, process payments, or issue refunds, credits, or vouchers.
